02 · Project Overview

Kozmo Portal is an Agentic AI-based enterprise workflow platform — designed to centralize workflow orchestration and improve collaboration across procurement and contract operations.

Before Kozmo, the customer's operational teams worked across half a dozen disconnected systems, each with its own approval routing and document conventions. The platform's mandate was clear: collapse the fragmentation into a single AI-assisted surface where intake, routing, and approval all happened with shared visibility.

As the lead Business Analyst, I shaped the discovery process, translated stakeholder language into engineering-ready specifications, and ran the agile cadence that took the platform from whiteboard to enterprise rollout.

03 · Business Problem

The friction was structural.

Operational teams were paying a tax in coordination, duplication, and waiting — every single day.

Manual workflows across disconnected systems with no shared source of truth.

Poor end-to-end workflow visibility for managers and approvers.

Inconsistent approval processes — varying by team and by request type.

Delayed operational turnaround on time-sensitive procurement requests.

No standardized workflow documentation — institutional knowledge living in inboxes.

High dependency on manual coordination across cross-functional teams.

11 · Challenges & Resolutions

The real work was alignment.

Challenge 01

Stakeholders spoke different operational dialects.

Procurement, legal and engineering used overlapping terms with subtly different meanings — generating ambiguity in every spec.

Resolution

Built a shared glossary as a first-class artifact.

Anchored every BRD to a single, versioned glossary. Reviews moved from semantic debates to actual decisions.

Challenge 02

Workflow rules conflicted between tenants.

What one customer treated as required, another treated as optional — making a single configuration impossible.

Resolution

Introduced reusable templates with overrides.

Templates carried defaults; tenants overrode at the field level. Configuration time dropped 40% per onboarding.

Challenge 03

Scope crept invisibly between sprints.

Stakeholder feedback was being applied directly to stories mid-sprint, eroding velocity predictability.

Resolution

Stood up a triage rhythm with steering review.

All scope deltas were triaged, sized and steered weekly. Sprint commitments stabilized inside two cycles.

12 · Final Outcome

Kozmo Portal moved enterprise operations from fragmented coordination to a single AI-assisted surface — and gave delivery a rhythm it didn't have before.

Beyond the headline metric — 40% configuration effort reduction — the deeper win was structural: standardized workflows, shared visibility, and a backlog rhythm that survived stakeholder turnover. The platform became the team's operating system, not just a tool.
