02 · Project Overview

A full-lifecycle Contract Lifecycle Management platform — managing creation, approvals, e-signatures, execution, and reporting across enterprise contract operations.

Contract operations were spread across mailboxes, shared drives, and a brittle import tool that worked when nobody was watching it. The customer needed one platform that could absorb their existing volume, automate the routine, and surface the exceptions to the right humans — with the reporting their executives kept asking for.

As lead Business Analyst, I drove discovery, designed the lifecycle workflows end-to-end, specified the import validation engine, and partnered with engineering on the Power BI reporting layer that turned operational data into executive answers.

03 · Business Problem

Contracts were everywhere and nowhere.

The legal and procurement teams managed thousands of agreements, but couldn't answer a single executive question without a manual sweep.

Fragmented contract systems with no single source of truth.

Manual approvals — emails, screenshots, and missed sign-offs.

Delayed execution cycles, especially around quarter-end pressure.

Limited reporting visibility — KPIs hand-built in spreadsheets.

Inconsistent import validation, allowing dirty data to enter the system.

High manual tracking effort across renewals, expirations and obligations.

11 · Challenges & Resolutions

Three knots. Three untanglings.

Challenge 01

Legacy contract data was wildly inconsistent.

The import tool kept failing on records that "looked fine" — bad dates, mismatched parties, orphan amendments.

Resolution

Specified a tiered validation engine.

Hard rules blocked imports; soft rules flagged for review. 60+ recurring data issues classified, owned and resolved.

Challenge 02

Reporting kept lagging behind business questions.

Each new executive question required a one-off SQL pull and a manual Excel rebuild.

Resolution

Power BI semantic layer + curated KPIs.

Defined a stable KPI dictionary backed by a Power BI dataset. Reporting time dropped from days to live.

Challenge 03

Approvers gamed the workflow.

Reviewers were rubber-stamping at quarter-end to clear the queue, hiding meaningful exceptions.

Resolution

Risk-tiered routing + audit trail.

Higher-risk contracts now require explicit, attributable sign-off with comments — surfaced on the dashboard.

12 · Final Outcome

eContracts & Import Tool turned a scattered estate of agreements into a single, auditable, executive-visible operation — handling 500+ contracts a month with reporting that finally answered the right questions.

The numbers — 60% manual reporting reduction, 3+ hours/week saved, 60+ data issues resolved — only tell part of the story. The deeper shift was cultural: contract operations stopped being a black box and started being a managed function.
