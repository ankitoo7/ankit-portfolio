02 · Project Overview

An AI-powered procurement and contract intake platform — built to simplify vendor onboarding, workflow approvals, and operational validation at enterprise scale.

Procurement teams were drowning in repetitive intake. Vendors filled out long forms, finance re-keyed the data into a separate system, legal hunted for clauses, and approvals bounced through email chains. The Intake Agent's job was to absorb that chaos and resolve it into a single, auditable flow.

As lead Business Analyst, I owned the discovery, the rule-set, and the fit/gap analysis between three legacy systems and the new agent — the work that decided what the AI would automate and what humans would still touch.

03 · Business Problem

Intake was the bottleneck.

The procurement org wasn't slow because of approvals — it was slow because every request started life as a partially-filled form.

Manual vendor onboarding with hand-keyed data across multiple systems.

Repetitive data entry by both vendors and internal procurement teams.

Approval bottlenecks — requests stalling for days at a single reviewer.

Inconsistent intake workflows varying by region and category.

Poor intake visibility — no shared status across procurement, legal & finance.

Operational inefficiencies compounded by handoff delays.

11 · Challenges & Resolutions

Teaching the agent to doubt itself.

Challenge 01

Rules conflicted between regions.

What APAC procurement treated as standard, EMEA escalated for legal review — the agent kept making locally-correct, globally-wrong calls.

Resolution

Region as a first-class rule dimension.

Re-modeled rules with region scoped at the policy level. Conflict rate dropped sharply within a single sprint.

Challenge 02

Stakeholders distrusted "the AI" by default.

Reviewers were over-correcting confident AI decisions because the agent felt opaque.

Resolution

Surfaced reasoning in plain language.

Every decision shipped with a "why" snippet referencing the underlying rule. Override rate normalized.

Challenge 03

Legacy data was inconsistent across systems.

Same vendor, three identities. Data mapping kept breaking on edge cases nobody owned.

Resolution

Defined a canonical schema + reconciliation rules.

Every legacy field mapped to one canonical attribute, with explicit owner and tie-breaker logic.

12 · Final Outcome

The Intake Agent moved procurement from a queue into a flow — vendors onboarded faster, reviewers focused on edge cases, and leadership finally had visibility.

Beyond the 40% manual reduction, the deeper outcome was operational: an AI you could audit. Every decision was explainable, every rule was versioned, and every escalation was deliberate. That made it safe for the business to lean on.
